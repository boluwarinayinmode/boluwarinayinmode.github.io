I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at the University of California, Davis, advised by Professor Cindy Rubio-González. Previously, I received my B.Sc. (Hons) in Computer Science from the University of Ibadan.

My research interests span software reliability and the automated detection, reproduction, and repair of software bugs, usually by leveraging large language models (LLMs). Currently, I am focused on autonomously reproducing and fixing scalability bugs in large-scale distributed systems such as Apache Cassandra and Kafka. These bugs are interesting because they stay latent at small scale and only emerge during large-scale deployment. They also manifest as a range of symptoms that degrade system performance rather than as outright failures, so reproduction and repair success cannot be evaluated with the traditional fail-pass oracle, where the bug-reproducing test must fail on the buggy version of the code and pass on the fixed one. I am also interested in scalability bugs in LLM inference and serving infrastructure such as vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, and Ray Serve. These are the software stacks that put LLMs into production at scale, and they now serve the LLMs that power AI services, which makes building tools to keep them reliable and prevent failures increasingly important.
